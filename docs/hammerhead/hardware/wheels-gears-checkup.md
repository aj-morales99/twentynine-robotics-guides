# Wheels & gears check-up

<span class="status-chip ready">Ready</span>

Hammerhead and Mini Hunter use a similar wheel, inner-rim, flange-coupling, and geared-motor arrangement. Perform this inspection before a match and whenever a wheel feels loose.

<div class="know-fact know-fact--danger" role="note">
  <span class="know-fact__label">Before touching the wheels</span>
  <p>Stop the robot, disconnect its battery, and place it on a stable stand. Keep the RC transmitter switched off while inspecting the drivetrain.</p>
</div>

## 1. Check each wheel for looseness

1. Hold one wheel and rotate it gently while the robot remains stationary.
2. Rock the wheel lightly along the motor shaft and from side to side.
3. Watch where the black inner rim meets the motor shaft.

Normal geared-motor play can produce a small amount of movement. Service the wheel when the rim itself slides, wobbles, or turns loosely on the shaft.

## 2. Remove only the silicone tire

1. Hold the black 3D-printed inner rim steady.
2. Stretch and slide the silicone tire evenly off the rim.
3. Leave the inner rim, flange coupling, motor shaft, gearbox, and motor mount installed.

Do not pull the complete rim away from the motor and do not pry against the gearbox.

## 3. Tighten the flange-coupling set screw

1. Rotate the inner rim until an access hole aligns with the coupling set screw.
2. Insert the supplied Allen key fully into the screw socket.
3. Turn gently to the right until the rim no longer slips on the shaft.
4. Do not overtighten. Replace a rounded set screw or damaged Allen key instead of forcing it.

If removable medium-strength threadlocker is available, use only a tiny amount on a replacement set screw. Keep it away from the rim, tire, motor, bearings, and gearbox.

## 4. Check the gearbox and motor response

Rotate each wheel slowly by hand and compare the two sides. Stop and inspect further if one side:

- Binds at the same point during every rotation
- Produces grinding or repeated skipping sounds
- Has noticeably greater resistance than the other side
- Rubs against the motor mount or robot body
- Allows the gearbox or motor to move inside its mount

Do not open the gearbox during a routine pre-match inspection. Contact Twentynine Robotics if grinding, repeated skipping, a bent shaft, or a loose motor mount remains after the wheel is secured.

## 5. Reinstall and test

1. Stretch the silicone tire evenly back over the inner rim.
2. Confirm that it sits flat and centered.
3. Repeat the inspection on the other wheel.
4. Reconnect the battery and perform the first powered test with both wheels raised.
5. Confirm that both sides start, stop, and change direction evenly.

<div class="know-fact know-fact--success" role="note">
  <span class="know-fact__label">Ready to use</span>
  <p>Both wheels should remain centered, avoid rubbing, stay secure on their shafts, and respond evenly during the supported low-speed test.</p>
</div>
