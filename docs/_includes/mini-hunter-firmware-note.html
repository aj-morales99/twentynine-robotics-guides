Before you start: Install the latest compatible firmware for your Mini Hunter and follow the guide for the version you install. Mini Hunter V2.7 and earlier must be updated to Sinyas-compatible V2.8 firmware before using Sinyas. If you keep the older firmware, use its Bluetooth Electronics guide.

To request the right firmware, email twentyninerobotics@gmail.com or message the official Twentynine Robotics page on Facebook. Include whether your robot is 1 kg or 3 kg and its current firmware version if you know it.
