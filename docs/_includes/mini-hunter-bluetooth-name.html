Find your Mini Hunter in the Bluetooth list. The usual format is MiniHunterV2.8_####. For example, MiniHunterV2.8_0082 breaks down like this:

  • MiniHunter is the robot name.
  • V2.8 is the firmware and design version. V2.8 is the latest.
  • 0082 is the robot’s four-digit serial number. Look for your own robot’s number, including any leading zeros.

The Bluetooth name helps you select the right robot. Sinyas confirms the Mini Hunter profile when the board replies after connecting.
